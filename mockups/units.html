Decisions the mockup takes

  • Stored in AC's own units, always. Car figures, track lengths, ballast, temperatures and a championship's race distances stay kilometres, kilograms and Celsius on disk; only what is shown and typed converts. Nothing persisted changes, and a championship file reads the same on every machine.
  • A typed number is in the units shown. A race distance typed as 500 miles is kept as 804.672 km and reads back as 500 mi. Steppers and sliders over a figure AC wants whole - ballast in 5 kg steps, air in whole °C - keep their steps and show each one converted (5 kg reads as 11 lb), rather than taking a lb figure that would have to be rounded behind the player's back.
  • 0-100 km/h reads as 0-62 mph in UK and US. It is the same measurement, and a car's author never states a 0-60 time to convert to.
  • Power stays bhp. Every car's data states it, the game's own UI shows it, and kW or PS would be a fourth question nobody asked.
  • Lap times, gaps and money never change - none of them is a unit choice.
  • The game is left alone. Its speedometer reads whatever the player set in Assetto Corsa.

To decide

  • A or B. A is one choice beside Theme and Language, three tiles each previewing what it reads as - the UK tile is what makes one choice enough, since that mix (mph and miles, kg and °C) is the one a two-way metric/imperial switch gets wrong. B is a choice per quantity: any mix, at the price of four rows of segments. Recommended: A.
  • New installs start on Metric, or on whatever Windows' region says (US region to US, UK to UK).
