Prize money
One purse editor, used twice. A purse is generated from three answers - how much it pays in all, how many places it pays, and the shape of the curve - and every place can then be adjusted by hand. The championship has one purse for each race and one for the season; a class either shares them or pays its own, using the very same editor on its Prize money tab. Everything below is live: change the pot, the places or the shape and the table regenerates; type into a place and it is marked as adjusted.
