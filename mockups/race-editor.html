2.1 One class, one panel. The Cars and AI tabs are gone, and everything is in one column: name, cars, then drivers.
  • 2.1.2 The two ways of naming cars are now Spec and List, under the label Cars from. The field beside them is labelled Spec.
  • 2.1.3 The spec field is the height of any other field: the spec's name, its class and how many of its cars are installed, with a chevron. Clicking it opens the spec picker. The cars show once, below it.
  • From a list, "Add a car" is a dashed tile the size of a car, first in the same grid, and each car has its ✕ on the picture.
  • 2.1.4 AI strength and aggression:
    • Each shows the figure the field actually races at, large, with the sum that makes it: your difficulty, then the race's step, then the class's step.
    • A bar from 0 to 100 marks that figure and the band the drivers spread across. Each driver sits up to 2 either side of the class.
    • The stepper stops where the figure would leave 0-100: + is disabled once the result reaches 100.
    • The "How much stronger…" hint is gone.

2.1.3 A spec picker you can search. It replaces the one long select of 100+ specs with a dialog shaped like the car browser.
  • Search matches spec names and the cars in them, so typing "488" finds every spec the Ferrari 488 races in.
  • The rail lists the classes (Formula 4, GT3, LMP2…) grouped by discipline, each with how many of its specs you can race. "Recently used" sits at the top.
  • Each spec is a card: its name, three of its cars, and how many of them are installed, as a count and a bar. A spec with nothing installed is dimmed and sorts last.
  • "Installed only" is a switch beside a shorter search box, on by default, so specs with no installed cars are hidden.
  • The championship editor's spec field would switch to the same dialog.

2.2.2 Weather in two steps: what kind, then how much. The shared weather control is used by the race weekend and by the championship editor, so this redesigns both.
  • Six kinds, always visible with an icon and a name, in the weather colours the Settings allow-list already uses: Clear & clouds, Haze & fog, Rain, Storm, Snow & sleet, Other. On a narrow window they wrap to three a row and keep their labels.
  • Then that kind's types as one ordered strip, lightest to heaviest, with a bar under each showing how heavy it is. Rain is no longer hidden behind "More weathers", which is gone.
  • Types your Settings switch off stay in the strip, struck through, so you can see why they can't be picked.
  • A line on top says what is picked, in that kind's colour: "Light rain - wet track, RainFX". It is the clear confirmation the old menu never gave.
  • "Changes as it runs" keeps its own slot list. Each slot uses this same two-step picker in a popover.

2.2.1 A race weekend has sessions, not days. The Day headings and "Add day 2" are gone from the race weekend's list. Sessions are one list, dragged to reorder, with "Add a session" under them. The championship editor keeps its days.
  • 2.2.3 Position is a compact slider bounded by your class: from P1 to the number of cars in it, so it can't go past them. The big figure says "P5 in GT3", and − and ＋ nudge it by one.
  • The position counts within your class, the way you think about it, so P5 means fifth GT3 car on the grid. Overall the whole field still lines up on pace, class by class.
  • The slider is compact: a small figure and thumb, kept to the width of the controls above it.
