Weekend formats
Two directions, each shown twice: a format that has just been made - one day, one race - and a full three-day weekend with a session being dragged. A format grows a day at a time, to the three a weekend may span; removing a day moves its sessions onto the day before. A session's day is wherever it sits, so there is no day field. Every session carries its conditions: each group - weather, time, temperature, track, wind - either follows the round or is set for that session, the same controls the round's Conditions tab uses.
