Coloured stripes - every place, and what replaces each

Decided. #1 takes A (the map well), #2 the alternative (discipline named above the title), #3 the class dot, #4 A (the row tinted, cards plain). 5-18, 21 and 23 as proposed; 19, 20 and 22 stay as they are. All of that is built.

Round 3. Five more discipline bars turned up after building - #24-28 - and all five stay as they are.

24 places draw a coloured bar along one side of something, in five shapes: a left stripe on a card, a top stripe on a card, a note with a bar down its left, a row with an inset bar, and a loose vertical pill standing for a discipline. One constant draws a stripe nobody uses (MODE_STRIPE in lib/accents.ts) and just gets deleted.

Four replacements, picked per place by what the stripe was actually saying:

  • Drop - something else on the element already says it (a chip, a ringed mark, a tinted row). The stripe goes and nothing replaces it.
  • Tinted head - a card whose stripe names its kind (a contract, a sponsor) gets that colour as the fill of its header band, the band it already has.
  • Wash - the stripe's colour becomes a translucent background over the whole element, or over the map well a card already shows.
  • Mark - the colour moves into a small object that is the app's own vocabulary: a class dot, an icon well.
  • Callout - the five notes with a bar become one <Callout> component: a rounded tint with a leading icon, no bar.

Left out on purpose: things that only look like stripes but do a job - drag-and-drop insertion lines (SeatSheetRow, TeamTile, SessionList), the calendar's today line, underline tabs, gauge ticks, round progress segments and the ladder head's reach bar.
