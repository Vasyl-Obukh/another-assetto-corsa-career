Theme

Damage moves from the championship to the player. How much a crash costs the person driving is a setting of theirs, like ABS, so it joins the driving aids: set once in the app's settings, set apart in a career's own, and no longer a rule a championship or a race event writes down. Tyre wear, fuel use, slipstream and tyre blankets stay the championship's rules.

Decisions the mockup takes

  • A Damage group of its own between Electronics and Helpers: mechanical damage on the same slider the regulation had, with its Off, Half and Real stops and a tick where Real sits, and Visual damage moved beside it from Helpers - the two are one question, how much a crash shows and costs.
  • Real is the starting point, Assetto Corsa's own 100 %, so nothing changes for anyone until they move it.
  • A career follows the app's or sets its own, as every aid already does. Set apart, the tile says what the app has where the two differ ("App: 100%").
  • Quick races use the app's, as they use the app's other aids. The Race tab's rules lose the slider, since they are the same inputs as the editor's.
  • Championships stop stating it. 177 shipped championships write damage: 100, which is the default anyway; four write 50 - GT86 Cup, Hot Hatch Trophy, Lotus Cup and the MX-5 Mk1 Championship - and those go too. A user's championship file that still names damage is not refused: the key is simply never read.
  • The rating does not care. Damage moves no figure the career is rated or paid on.
