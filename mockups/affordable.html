Championships: "Can afford a round" - a switch in a Yours section at the top of the rail, the Store's own shape. Shown in a career only; a standalone championship has no money.
  • What a round costs = the next round's entry fee + the rent of the cheapest eligible car you have installed, or nothing for the car if the garage already holds an eligible one. Those are the two amounts a launch charges, so "affordable" here means the launch will not be refused.
  • Per rung, not per championship. Rent depends on the class's cars, so a multi-class championship can stay on its GT3 rung and drop off its prototype rung.
  • The balance is $8,400 here. Switching on keeps every rung whose round you can pay for and hides the rest; the pill and the count say how many are left, as every other filter does.
To decide: (1) A or B. B closes every card with what a round costs, red where you are short, and the breakdown on hover (shown open on Audi TT Cup with the switch off). The amount is money, so it stays neutral; only "short" takes a colour, and "In your garage" the success green. (2) One round, or the rest of the season? I recommend one round: it is the rule a launch is refused by, and prizes come in as you race.
