Calendar. The month header and Next event widget from B, then the filters, then a Days | Month switch.
  • The filters are the app's own: Status (All, Available, Entered, Locked) and the disciplines, as one row of controls above the view rather than a column beside it.
  • Days is A's timeline, with each event as a card so three or four fit side by side. The left date column and the today line stay.
  • Month is B's grid. Click a day to list its events beside the grid. With Narrow window on, that list opens as a panel sliding in from the right instead.
  • In Days, the month buttons and the arrows scroll to that month. In Month, they change the month shown.
Home. A new first tab. For now it holds C's two sections: your next weekend and your championships. The dashed boxes mark where later information could go, and are not part of this pass.
  • With nothing entered yet, the next-weekend card would point to the Calendar on Available.
  • Each championship row opens that championship's page. The bar shows one segment per round: raced, missed, next and to come.
