Other places that lose rounds. I went through every way the career can mark a round as a non-start. Two of them need the dialog; the other two are covered already or are up to you.
  • Needed - entering part-way through a season. Entering a championship whose first rounds are already behind the clock marks them missed straight away, and today nothing says so.
  • Needed - leaving a weekend part-way. Starting another round, or advancing, while a weekend has sessions left is refused today. With the dialog it could be allowed: the sessions not yet run become non-starts.
  • Already covered - withdrawing. This has its own confirmation, which says the remaining rounds become non-starts.
  • Not needed - the header's Go to and Home's Advance to. Both stop at your next round, so they never skip one.

The round page links to its championship. Two places it could go; you can pick one or both.
  • A - In the Back row. "← Calendar / Italian F4 Championship", like a breadcrumb. It stays pinned with Back as the page scrolls, and it's the route people expect.

My recommendation is A, and B is cheap to add on top.
