Recommended tracks. Shipped championships stop naming LFM's folders. A round names a recommended track - a real circuit's layout, such as Suzuka - Grand Prix - and each one races whichever installed track stands for it: one the app finds by itself from the folders it knows, or one the player chooses from every track installed, in the library or not. LFM can rename, add and drop layouts as often as it likes: a round only cares that something installed is Suzuka.

The concept

  1. A recommended track is a real circuit's layout, shipped as a catalogue. src/shared/constants/ gains the list: each circuit has a name, a nation, the layouts the library races (each with its real length); each layout lists the folders the app knows stand for it, best first - rt_suzuka/suzukagp_lfm_nodrs, then rt_suzuka/suzukagp. Ids are ours and readable: suzuka/gp. Adding a folder LFM renamed is one line in that list, and changes nothing anyone stored.
  2. A shipped round at a mod track names the recommended track, track: { recommended: 'suzuka/gp' }, beside today's { id, layout }, which every round at Kunos' own circuits keeps - those never move. A user's championship may name either; the editor saves a track that stands for a recommended one as the recommended one, so a championship exported to another machine finds that machine's Suzuka.
  3. Where Kunos ship the layout, their circuit is the fallback. Spa 2022, LFM's Monza, Imola 2025, Barcelona 2023, the OSRW Brands Hatch and Nurburgring and Zandvoort 2023 stay recommended, for the cars they hold. Without one installed, the round races Kunos' own - Kunos' version, never Not installed - and its grid is cut to Kunos' pit boxes. An older version of the same layout counts; a different layout does not - Silverstone International never stands for National.
  4. Which track a layout races is worked out, never stored, unless the player chose it. settings.trackChoices holds only what the player picked, as seriesActive holds only what they flipped. Otherwise it is the first folder the catalogue lists that is installed. Nothing found means Not installed, and the round is unavailable exactly as a missing track is today.
  5. Every row ends the same way: Choose…, and the menu wherever there is a track. The menu opens that track's page; on Your choice it also goes back to what the app would race by itself - the first known folder, else Kunos' version, else nothing - and on To confirm it says Not this one, which keeps the guess from coming back and leaves the row Not installed. That refusal is kept in settings.trackChoices beside the picks.
  6. Guessing is offered, never applied. For a layout with nothing found, an installed track whose name, country and length (within 2%) match is shown To confirm with Use it. A wrong guess would race a round on the wrong layout without a word, which is the silent kind of failure this project refuses.
  7. A track standing for a recommended one is in the library. Choosing a track from Your content's uncatalogued mods here does what switching it on there does. That is what lets DISTRIBUTOR_TRACKS and MOD_TRACKS go: no shipped championship names an LFM folder any more, LFM's switch in Content sources counts for its cars alone, and the known gap "a mod's layout inside a base circuit reads as base" closes for every shipped championship - spa/2022_lfm_standing is simply Spa-Francorchamps, 2022 on.
  8. Resolved when a round is lowered, so a season's own copy names the recommended track. Installing Suzuka mid-season makes its round raceable; a round part-way through keeps the race the slot already holds. Results, history and the calendar name the circuit, which reads the same whatever the folder is called. Distances still become laps off the installed layout's own length, and grids are still capped by its pit boxes.

Where it shows

  • Settings - Tracks, a tab of its own after Content sources: every circuit as a card, a row per layout, searchable and filtered by state. The card is one component, RecommendedTrackCard, reused below.
  • Where to get them is said once, at the head of the Tracks tab: Low Fuel Motorsport and the community track list, which between them hold nearly every circuit here. No card or row carries a link of its own.
  • A round that cannot be raced - its page's SkipPanel names the missing track with Choose a track and Where to get it, which opens the Tracks tab, beside Skip as today.
  • "Track not installed" on a championship's Rounds tab and the editor's Calendar tab becomes a button opening the card in a dialog, with the same Where to get it under it.
  • The championship library's filter rail already says what a championship needs; a missing recommended track takes the place of a missing LFM layout there with no new UI.
  • First-run setup gains Content sources and Tracks as steps 3 and 4, between the scan and Make it yours, each the Settings tab's own body. Neither blocks: a step reads Skip until something on it is changed. Content sources comes first, since switching a pack off changes which championships, and so which tracks, matter.
  • Content sources switch on, not off. Every DLC pack, mod team and LFM starts off, in setup and in Settings alike, and the player switches on what they have; only the base game is always offered. settings.hiddenContentSources becomes offeredContentSources, holding what is switched on, so anything the catalogue gains later arrives off too. A row's status reads the same either way - "Needed by 6 championships" - with no amber, since off is where every row starts. Your own settings.json needs nothing deleted: the old key is ignored, so you switch on what you have once.
  • Content sources loses half a sentence. With no circuit credited to a mod team or LFM, the mods footer stops at "hides the championships whose cars need it"; "rounds at its circuits are only skipped" goes. LFM's own page there becomes /lfmapp too.

What follows once this is agreed

  • No per-circuit link research. The two links live in src/shared/constants/ beside the packs' and mod teams' pages. LFM's is https://lowfuelmotorsport.com/lfmapp.
  • Rework the calendars. With Monaco, Suzuka, Hockenheim, Albert Park, Paul Ricard, Baku, Marina Bay, Losail and the rest recommended, the rounds missing-tracks.md places get added to their championships - Monaco alone completes 44 - and every round now on an LFM folder moves to its recommended track, the seven with Kunos behind them included. Silverstone rounds from the 1950s to the 1980s race International. The Formula E seasons gain their missing rounds as the circuits arrive. missing-tracks.md is then regenerated against the recommended list, so it lists only what nobody has built.
  • The content cache has to be deleted once the catalogue drops the LFM track entries.

Decided

  1. Period layouts were kept by accident and are gone, Magny-Cours before 2003 with them. Formula E is added instead: nine street and Formula E circuits, plus a Formula E layout at Ricardo Tormo and Misano, and VRC's Mexico layout named as the Formula E one it is. Monaco needs no row of its own: the E-Prix has raced the Grand Prix layout since 2021.
  2. Kunos as the fallback for the seven, which stay recommended for their pit boxes - point 3 of the concept.
  3. Portland has its Formula E layout only.
  4. LFM leaves the track catalogue.
  5. Kunos' circuits stay off the page.
  6. First run: Content sources and Tracks become optional setup steps, drawn above.
